# PitHub Team

## 1. Team Overview

PitHub is a university-focused video and document-sharing platform designed to allow authorized users to securely upload, organize, manage, and share instructional videos and documents.

Our team consists of five members who collaboratively design, develop, test, document, and maintain the PitHub application.

Our team follows Scrum while maintaining a collaborative, self-organizing structure. All team members contribute to the development of the product. The Scrum Master and Product Owner also perform development responsibilities in addition to their Scrum-specific responsibilities.

# 6. Team Collaboration Guidelines

Our team will work collaboratively and support one another throughout each sprint.

Our team members are responsible for:

- Communicating regularly with our team.
- Completing assigned sprint work.
- Participating in Scrum events.
- Reviewing teammates' work.
- Keeping documentation updated.
- Reporting blockers early.
- Maintaining the quality and security of PitHub.

Our team will:

1. Respect each member's contributions and ideas.
2. Discuss major technical decisions before implementation when practical.
3. Review pull requests before merging significant changes.
4. Avoid modifying another member's work without communication.
5. Document important architectural and implementation decisions.
6. Prioritize working software and maintainable code.

# 9. Definition of Done

A Product Backlog item is considered **Done** when the agreed-upon criteria have been satisfied and the work meets our team's quality expectations.

At minimum, the Definition of Done requires:

- The work described by the story has been completed.
- The implementation has been tested appropriately.
- Necessary documentation has been updated.
- The completed work has been integrated into the product.
- The change has been reviewed and merged into the appropriate branch.
- At least one GitHub commit exists on the product branch with a commit message following: Story [[STORY-NUMBER]] done
